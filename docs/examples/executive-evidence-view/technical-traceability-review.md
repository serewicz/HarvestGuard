## What the review must establish

A reviewer **independent of the #152 implementation**, reviewing the immutable
committed artifacts in [`samples/`](samples/) (not the code under review, and
not artifacts regenerated by the reviewer), must resolve **every substantive
statement** in the evaluated output to exactly one of:

- an exact stored evidence occurrence, identified by scan ID, ordinal and
  finding ID;
- a named evidence check;
- a named bounded technical derivation; or
- a standing documented limitation.

Required, per the issue contract:

- Duplicate finding IDs stay separately traceable by ordinal.
- Every status reason resolves to its named checks or exceptions.
- Every exception resolves to the relevant occurrence or policy basis.
- Every observation and conclusion retains its nature, method, references and
  limitations.
- Unknowns and limitations are not omitted.
- No unrecognized field **value** is exposed anywhere.
- Withheld field names and the withholding/local-retention disclosure agree
  between JSON and Markdown.
- Unsupported statements are recorded as **failures**. The reviewer must not
  rationalize or silently repair an unsupported claim.

An AI reviewer (for example Codex) may serve as the independent technical
reviewer, provided it reviews immutable artifacts produced by the actual
implementation, records its method and limitations, and did not implement the
code under review. That is category 2 evidence and must never be described as
human validation.
