# Participant response form (template)

Copy this form per participant. Fill in **only** the fields below: no name, no
contact details, no employer, no demographics, no recordings.

Do not use this form until the maintainer has approved and frozen the protocol
in [`comprehension-protocol.md`](comprehension-protocol.md).
